About the workshop

A week of short talks linking researchers at the University of Queensland and the University of Exeter who work on the mathematics and statistics of extreme weather.

Each session is streamed live between the two campuses, so Brisbane meets in the evening and Exeter in the morning of the same day. Monday to Thursday are fifteen minute overview talks pitched to a mixed audience across mathematics, statistics and climate science. Friday is an online day of open research discussion in Zoom breakout rooms, each dedicated to a topic. We welcome people at every career stage, from HDR students and postdocs through to senior researchers and those working in industry.

  • Extremes in dynamical systemsExtreme value theory for deterministic and random dynamics.
  • Return times and recurrenceHow often extreme events come back, and how long we wait for them.
  • Machine learning for climate extremesSpatio-temporal methods for rare events in climate data.
  • Consecutive and compound eventsHeatwaves, persistent rainfall and co-occurring hazards under a changing climate.
